About this archive

These tools take you from the end of aviation's Golden Age — the final years of Pan Am, Eastern and TWA — through the beginning of post-9/11 consolidation, the birth of superhubs and the regional jet takeover. See the coverage table below for more details on what's included.

This data was compiled by digitizing original copies of physical airline timetables, in addition to data first digitized on AirTimes.com and DepartedFlights.com. Visit AirTimes and Departed Flights to view supplemental route maps, additional timetables, aircraft photos, and original data.
